PET PARENT FAQs

The stuff you're probably wondering.

How many Sploot vet clinics are in Denver?
Sploot operates several Denver vet clinics across the metro — including Highlands, LoHi, Cherry Creek, RiNo, Wash Park, and Sloan's Lake — plus nearby locations across the Front Range. The full, up-to-date list is on the Denver locations map above.
Does Sploot offer urgent care vet services in Denver?
Yes. Every Denver Sploot location offers urgent care and same-day sick visits during regular hours, plus walk-in care and urgent drop-offs. We handle the vast majority of urgent concerns in-house — including vomiting, limping, ear infections, foxtails, and skin or eye irritation.
Are same-day appointments available at Denver Sploot locations?
Yes — same-day appointments are available daily at all Denver Sploot vet clinics. Check today’s availability for the next open slot, or call (720) 743-6869 and we'll find you one.
How do I book an appointment at a Sploot Denver vet clinic?
You can book online in under a minute, call (720) 743-6869, or message us in the Sploot Vets app. Membership isn't required — anyone can book.
What areas of Denver does Sploot serve?
Our Denver vet clinics serve pet parents across the city and surrounding Front Range — including Highlands, LoHi, Cherry Creek, RiNo, Wash Park, Sloan's Lake, Sunnyside, Berkeley, Capitol Hill, Stapleton/Central Park, and nearby Centennial and Colorado Springs.
What hours are Sploot's Denver vet clinics open?
All Denver Sploot vet clinics are open 8am–8pm, 365 days a year — including weekends and holidays. Same-day appointments and urgent drop-offs welcome.
Is Sploot a full-service animal hospital in Denver?
Yes — Sploot is a full-service Denver animal hospital offering primary care, urgent care & sick visits, surgery, dental, vaccinations, puppy & kitten care, and more, all under one roof. If your pet ever needs specialty or critical care, we coordinate the next step for you.
Do I need to be a member to book an appointment at Sploot?
No — membership is optional. Anyone can book a visit at any Denver Sploot vet clinic. Our SplootPack membership is available for pet parents who want bundled wellness, exam, and discount perks year-round.
