PET PARENT FAQs

The stuff you're probably wondering.

Where is Sploot Highlands and how do I get there?
Sploot Highlands is at 3774 Julian St in Denver — on Julian between W 37th and W 38th Ave, two blocks east of Tennyson Street. The entrance is directly off Julian. Get directions →
Is there parking at the Highlands clinic?
Yes — free street parking is available on Julian St and W 38th Ave. The clinic entrance is wheelchair-accessible. If you're combining your visit with a Tennyson Street stop, it's a short walk away.
What neighborhoods does Sploot Highlands serve?
We're the closest Sploot clinic for pet parents in Highlands, LoHi, Sunnyside, Berkeley, Sloan's Lake, Jefferson Park, and West Highland. Nearby Sploot clinics — RiNo, Commons Park, and 9+CO — are also less than 15 minutes away.
What services does Sploot Highlands offer?
Sploot Highlands is a full-service clinic: wellness & preventive care, urgent care & sick visits, surgery, dental, vaccinations, puppy & kitten care, and more — all under one roof. We handle over 95% of your pet's care needs in-house.
Can I drop in for urgent care without an appointment?
Yes. Sploot Highlands accepts urgent drop-offs during regular hours (8am–8pm) for issues like vomiting, limping, ear infections, foxtails, skin and eye irritation, and altitude-related concerns. Call ahead at (720) 743-6869 so we can prep for your pet's arrival.
How do I book an appointment at the Highlands clinic?
Three ways: book online in under a minute, call (720) 743-6869, or message us in the Sploot Vets app. Same-day appointments are available daily — check today's availability for the next open slot.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring any prior medical records or vaccination history if you have them — we can also request them from your previous vet. A leash or carrier, your pet's favorite treats, and a list of any medications they're currently on are all helpful. New pet parents get $50 off the first visit with code FIRST50DEN.
Do you handle after-hours emergencies?
Sploot Highlands is open 8am–8pm, 365 days a year, including weekends and holidays — same-day urgent care is available during those hours. We do not offer overnight 24-hour critical care. If your pet needs after-hours emergency care, we'll help coordinate the right specialty hospital or ER, and we can follow up with your pet the next morning.
Do I need a SplootPack membership to be seen?
No — membership is optional and anyone can book a visit at Sploot Highlands. SplootPack is available for pet parents who want bundled wellness exams, vaccines, and discounts on services year-round.
Do you accept pet insurance?
Yes. We don't bill insurance directly, but we provide itemized invoices that you can submit to any major pet insurance carrier (Trupanion, Healthy Paws, Nationwide, ASPCA, Lemonade, and more) for reimbursement. Payment is collected at the visit — we accept all major cards, HSA/FSA, and CareCredit.
