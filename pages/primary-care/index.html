Common questions

Primary care, answered.

Do I need to commit to Sploot as my pet's regular vet to come in?

No commitment required. You're welcome to come in for a single visit — a wellness exam, a vaccine update, or a sick visit — and decide from there. Many of our clients start with one appointment and stay because of the experience, not because they signed up.

How do I transfer records from my old vet?

Easiest path: send us your previous vet's name and clinic when you book, and we'll request the records on your behalf. If you'd rather handle it directly, your old clinic can email or fax the records — we'll have them in your pet's chart before the first visit.

Do you accept pet insurance?

Yes. We don't bill insurance directly, but we'll provide a detailed itemized invoice — most major insurers (Trupanion, Healthy Paws, Embrace, Nationwide, ASPCA, Pets Best, Lemonade) accept this for reimbursement. Ask the front desk and we'll walk you through it.

What payment options do you accept?

All major credit cards, debit, HSA/FSA cards, and CareCredit. For larger procedures, we offer financing through Scratchpay and CareCredit so you can spread payments over 6–24 months. We also provide upfront estimates so you know what to expect before any treatment begins.

How do I get same-day or after-hours guidance?

Call us during open hours (8am–8pm, every day) and we'll triage over the phone — most issues we can either book the same day or guide you to the right next step. Outside of those hours, our voicemail provides instructions and the nearest 24/7 ER if your pet's situation can't wait until morning.
