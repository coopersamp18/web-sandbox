What emergency conditions can we treat?

We can handle many urgent and emergency situations. If you're unsure, call us and we'll guide you immediately.

Sudden illness & symptoms

Treatment for vomiting, diarrhea, fever, and other urgent symptoms. We can stabilize, run diagnostics, and start treatment the same day — or refer you onward if the case needs ICU care.

Injuries & trauma
Care for wounds, limping, and initial trauma evaluation.
Urinary & internal emergencies
Including blocked cats, infections, and abdominal pain.
Emergency surgeries
Bloat (GDV), pyometra, C-sections, exploratory surgery.
Diagnostics & stabilization
X-rays, bloodwork, ultrasound, IV fluids, medications.
When you should go directly to a 24/7 ER
Breathing issues, collapse, severe trauma, ICU-level needs.
