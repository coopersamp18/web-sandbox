Dental
Biscuitwith Sarah K. · Denver LoHi

The cleaning we'd put off for two years

Biscuit was 7 and had never had her teeth cleaned — partly because every other vet wanted to put her under for X-rays first. Sploot walked us through it, did the pre-op the same week, and by Thursday she was home with a clean mouth and the same goofy grin.

Their review

"Dr. Patel explained every step and never made me feel like I was being a worrier. Biscuit came home like nothing happened — and I finally stopped dreading her annual."

Anxious pets
Mochiwith Priya R. · Denver RiNo

The cat who used to hide for three days after a vet visit

Mochi spent every appointment hissing from inside her carrier — and the recovery was almost worse. Sploot's Fear Free room let us sit on the floor with her, do the exam at her pace, and skip the carrier muzzle entirely.

Their review

"I've never seen Mochi let a stranger touch her ears. Whatever they're doing here — the lighting, the snacks, the not-rushing — it works."

G Priya R. 1 month ago Posted to Google
Surgery
Otiswith Marcus T. · Cap Hill

A torn ACL on a Tuesday, walking again by Sunday

Otis is a 75-pound goof who tore his cruciate jumping off the porch. Sploot's surgeon called me three times before the operation — and twice the day after. I don't know any human doctor who does that.

Their review

"They sent me a video of him eating breakfast in the recovery suite. I cried. Best money I've ever spent on this dumb, beautiful dog."

G Marcus T. 6 weeks ago Posted to Google

Eight weeks old and already an honorary employee
